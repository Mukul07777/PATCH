Policy

 

Auto-fix starts an agent fix run when a scan finds new fixable findings at or above the threshold. It needs an LLM key, a GitHub or sample project, and for GitHub projects the Docker sandbox or remote repos allowed by an admin.

Notify these channels

New findings, failed scans and finished fix runs are sent to the ticked channels (all channels by default). Channels are managed in Settings.

Findings below the threshold, and ignored advisories, are shown as suppressed and left out of counts and the dashboard. Unrated findings are always reported.
